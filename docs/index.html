MicroProjects Eswatini E-Tendering System

A centralized, transparent digital procurement platform engineered to eliminate paper delays, enforce cryptographic bid integrity, and empower local contractors in community infrastructure projects.

Community Schools & Clinics

Direct procurement for rural development infrastructure across all 4 regions.

Clean Water Schemes & Bridges

Open tendering with standardized bills of quantities and published technical drawings.

Equitable Local Participation

Tailored support for Swazi SMMEs registered with the Construction Industry Council (CIC).

Cryptographic Bid Locking

At submission, every proposal is digested into an immutable SHA-256 hash. Bids remain sealed and tamper-evident until the official opening date.

Automated Deadlines

Server-side timers enforce strict tender closing at the second of the deadline. Late submissions are automatically blocked, eliminating favoritism.

Public Accountability

Evaluation rubrics, consensus score sheets, and final contract awardees are broadcast publicly to restore civic trust in state procurement.
